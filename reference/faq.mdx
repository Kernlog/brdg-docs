title: FAQ
description: Answers to common questions about integrating BRDG.
---

## Does BRDG hold my users' funds?

No. BRDG never holds funds or private keys. Your user's wallet signs every transaction.

## Do I need an account or an API key?

No. Every endpoint is open to anyone, subject to [rate limits](/api-reference/rate-limits). Partners that call BRDG from a server for many users can ask for an API key with higher limits.

## Why didn't the venue with the biggest number win?

Either a wallet cannot sign that venue's quote on this route (it shows `executable: false`), or its headline figure was higher than what actually arrives after fees. BRDG ranks on what arrives. See [Ranking](/concepts/ranking).

## Is the quoted amount what my user receives?

It is what the venue quoted, with the platform fee already taken off. `minAmountOutAtomic` is the least the transfer can deliver. If a different token arrives, the transfer ends as `PARTIAL`; check `receivedAsset` and `receivedAmountAtomic` on the transfer.

## What does `quote_drifted` mean?

If a quote is close to expiring when you build it, BRDG asks the venue for a fresh price first. If the price has moved more than the allowed amount, the build is refused and `details` contains both amounts. Get a new quote.

## My user signed but I never called submit. Where is the transfer?

It exists with the status `PENDING_SIGNATURE`, and you can read it by id. Submit the transaction hash to move it forward.

## Can I choose the venue?

Yes. Pass the `quoteId` of any executable quote to `POST /bridge/build`. It must come from the same quote response.

## What does a transfer cost?

Nothing, unless the chosen route beat the alternatives by more than 10 bps. Then the platform fee is 5 to 15 bps of the amount sent, capped at \$10, taken in the token being sent. Venue fees are included in every quoted amount. See [Fees](/concepts/fees).

## Is there a rate limit?

Yes. Each IP address and each sender wallet can make 10 quote requests, 10 build requests and 10 submit requests a minute. Reading markets and transfers is not limited. A `429` response includes `retryAfterMs`. See [Rate limits](/api-reference/rate-limits).

## How do I get higher rate limits?

Ask. Contact [@kernlog on Telegram](https://t.me/kernlog) or [kernlog@print.world](mailto:kernlog@print.world) with what you are building and the volume you expect. Partners get an API key, sent as `x-brdg-api-key`, that applies the per-IP limit to each of your users' `userIp` instead of your server, with a higher total limit for your key. See [Higher rate limits and partner access](/api-reference/rate-limits#higher-rate-limits-and-partner-access).

## How do I know when a transfer is done?

Poll `GET /bridge/transfers/{id}` until `status` is `COMPLETED`, `PARTIAL`, `REFUNDED`, `FAILED` or `ABANDONED`, or use `waitForTransfer` in the SDK. See [Track a transfer](/guides/track-a-transfer).
