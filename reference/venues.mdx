---
title: Venues
description: Every venue BRDG quotes, what kind of venue it is, and what it gives a wallet to sign.
---

BRDG integrates 36 venues. Every enabled venue that serves a route is asked for a quote, and all answers are ranked the same way. Venues can be switched off, or left out while they are unhealthy: [`GET /bridge/venues`](/api-reference/markets/venues) shows which are on right now, and [`GET /bridge/routes`](/api-reference/markets/routes) shows which serve a particular route.

In the table below, **ID** is the value of `venue` in API responses. **Type** is how the venue works. **Signs as** is what the venue gives a wallet to sign:

- **EVM**, **Solana**, **Tron** or **UTXO**: a transaction on that chain type.
- **Deposit address**: a plain transfer to an address the venue issues.
- **Signed intent**: an off-chain order the wallet signs.
