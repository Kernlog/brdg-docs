---
title: Referrals
description: Earn a share of every transfer you send through BRDG by adding two fields to the quote. No sign-up needed.
---

If you send transfers through BRDG, you can charge your own fee on top and have it paid straight to your wallet. There is nothing to register.

## Fields

Add both fields to `POST /bridge/quote`, or neither:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `referralWallet` | The wallet your share is paid to. It must be a valid address on the source chain. |
| `referralBps` | Your rate in basis points, from `0` to `15`. |

The referral carries over from the quote to `POST /bridge/build`, so you do not need to send it again.

```json
{
  "fromChain": "base",
  "toChain": "solana",
  "fromToken": "USDC",
  "toToken": "USDC",
  "amountAtomic": "100000000",
  "sender": "0x1111111111111111111111111111111111111111",
  "recipient": "3BQtHR41iDPiu9skeSVzmBDpZKcKqEEDnMn6UkbAHvZz",
  "referralWallet": "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
  "referralBps": 10
}
```

## How much you earn

Your share is charged on every transfer you refer, including transfers where BRDG charges no platform fee. It is added on top of the platform fee, never taken out of it.

Your share is capped at \$10 per transfer, converted into the token being sent at BRDG's price for it. This is separate from the platform's own \$10 cap. `fees.referral.amountAtomic` shows the amount after the cap.

## The wallet must match the source chain

Your share is paid on the source chain, so `referralWallet` must be valid there. An EVM address on a transfer from Solana is refused with `400 invalid_referral_wallet`, and a rate outside `0` to `15` with `400 invalid_referral_bps`. If you refer transfers from more than one kind of chain, keep one wallet for each, and pick the right one per quote.

## What your users see

The quote shows your share next to the platform fee, in the same token, before anything is signed:

```json
"fees": {
  "platform": { "bps": 5,  "amountAtomic": "50000",  "asset": "0x8335…02913", "tierSavedBps": 54 },
  "referral": { "bps": 10, "amountAtomic": "100000", "asset": "0x8335…02913", "wallet": "0x2222…2222" }
}
```

`referral` is `null` only when the quote named no referrer. `platform` can be `null` while `referral` is not: that transfer saved too little for a platform fee, and you are still paid.

## How you are paid

Your share is paid to your wallet in the same transaction the user signs for the bridge. On Solana it is an extra instruction in that transaction, and on EVM and Tron it is part of the `fee` step next to the bridge step. Nothing is held back and paid out later.

The one exception is a source chain where BRDG cannot collect fees at all. On those chains neither the platform fee nor your share is charged.

## Checking a payout

Each fee appears in the `fees` list of `GET /bridge/transfers/{id}`, with a `kind` of `platform` or `referral` and one of these statuses:

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `offered` | A `fee` step was built but not signed yet. |
| `expected` | The fee is inside a transaction the user signed. |
| `settled` | The transfer completed. `txHash` is the transaction that paid you. |
| `void` | No fee was collected: the transfer was refunded or failed, or the user never signed the `fee` step. |
