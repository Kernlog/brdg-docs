---
title: Fees
description: BRDG charges a platform fee only when it found your user a better price, and never more than $10 a transfer.
---

BRDG only charges when the route it found is meaningfully better than the alternatives. Most transfers pay no platform fee at all.

## Rate

When a route is chosen, BRDG compares it with the four next-best quotes a wallet could sign and works out how much better it is, on average, in basis points (bps). That saving sets the fee:

| Average saving | Platform fee |
| --- | --- |
| 10 bps or less | None |
| More than 10, up to 100 bps | 5 bps |
| More than 100, up to 120 bps | 10 bps |
| More than 120 bps | 15 bps |

Only the four closest competitors count, so a venue with a very poor price cannot push the fee up. If there is nothing to compare against, for example because only one venue serves the route, there is no platform fee. In that case `fees.platform` is `null` and each quote's `feeBps` is `0`.

## Maximum

The platform fee on a single transfer is capped at \$10, whatever the rate. BRDG converts the cap into the token you are sending at its own price for that token, and `feeAmountAtomic` shows the amount after the cap. If BRDG has no price for the token, the cap cannot be applied and the rate alone is charged.

A referral fee has its own, separate \$10 cap. See [Referrals](/concepts/referrals).

## What you see in a quote

Every output amount in a quote already has the fee taken off, so the number you show your user is the number they receive. When you build a quote, `expectedOutAtomic` matches the `amountOutAtomic` you were shown.

The fee is disclosed in the quote before anything is signed:

```json
"fees": {
  "platform": { "bps": 5, "amountAtomic": "50000", "asset": "0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913", "tierSavedBps": 55 },
  "referral": null
}
```

`bps` and `amountAtomic` are measured in the token you send, and `tierSavedBps` is the saving that set the rate. A `null` entry means no fee of that kind is charged. Each quote in `quotes` also carries its own `feeBps`, `feeAmountAtomic` and `tierSavedBps`, because each one saved a different amount.

## How the fee is collected

The fee comes out of the transfer itself. Your user never pays it in a second token. The `feeCapture` field on the build response tells you how:

| `feeCapture` | How it works | Signatures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `venue-param` | The venue takes the fee as part of its own transaction. Some venues take it from the output rather than the input. | 1 |
| `merged` | The fee transfer is an extra instruction inside the Solana transaction. | 1 |
| `batch` | A separate `fee` step comes before the bridge step. `feeBatch` lists the steps a wallet with EIP-5792 batching can sign as one bundle. | 1, or 2 without batching |
| `none` | No fee is collected on this transfer. | Not applicable |

If a Solana transaction has no room for the extra instruction, BRDG drops the fee rather than the transfer, and `feeCapture` is `none`.

## Hyperliquid deposits through Arbitrum

Some deposits to Hyperliquid bridge to Arbitrum first, and BRDG's relayer then credits the Hyperliquid account. The relayer charges its gas cost plus 10 cents, capped at \$1. This charge is already taken off the quoted output and needs no extra approval. See [Hyperliquid](/concepts/hyperliquid).
