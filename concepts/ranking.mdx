---
title: Ranking
description: How BRDG decides which quote is best.
---

## Ranked by what arrives

Quotes for the same token pair are ranked by net output, in the token's smallest unit, after every venue fee and the platform fee. For an exact-output transfer, where you fix the amount received, they are ranked by the input needed instead.

BRDG does not use a price feed to compare venues, so a stale or wrong price cannot change which route wins.

Each quote includes the figures the ranking used: `amountOutAtomic`, `minAmountOutAtomic`, `fees`, `feeBps` and `tierSavedBps`.

## Only signable routes can win

A quote is **eligible** when the venue serves the route and the token, BRDG's information about the venue is up to date, and the amount is within the venue's limits. It is **executable** when the venue can also produce a transaction or signature that a wallet on the source chain can sign.

`best` is the highest-ranked quote that is both. A quote that has a price but cannot be signed stays in `quotes` with its real figures and `executable: false`, so you can see it, but it never becomes `best`.

## Best price and fastest

| Field | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| `bestByPrice` | The `quoteId` of `best`. |
| `bestByTime` | The `quoteId` of the executable quote with the lowest `estimatedSeconds`. |

Speed never breaks a tie on price. A venue that does not give an estimated time cannot be `bestByTime`, so `null` there means no venue stated a time, not that the transfer is instant. Both fields are `null` when no quote is executable.

To build the fastest route instead of the cheapest, pass the `bestByTime` value as `quoteId` to `POST /bridge/build`.

## Venue data stays current

BRDG refreshes each venue's chains, tokens, routes and limits in the background, so quoting never waits on them. A venue whose data is out of date, or which is failing, is left out of quotes until it recovers, rather than being priced against limits that may have changed.

## Large transfers

A venue that pays best on a small amount can pay worst on a large one. Above a certain size for each token, the quote also measures how each venue's price changes with size. If splitting the transfer across several venues would beat the best single route, the response includes a `splitPlan` with the proposed allocation and its `improvementBps`. When no plan is offered, `planReason` says why. The ranked quotes, and what `POST /bridge/build` executes, stay the same either way.
